We walked into a SaaS company mid restructure: support drowning in noise, customer knowledge scattered across tools, briefing prep eating the better part of an hour per customer. The instinct was to hire, but the bottleneck was operational architecture, not headcount.

In one month we rebuilt how the work got done, with workflows that triaged the noise before a human saw it, drafts that wrote themselves from real customer data, and internal tools that closed loops nobody had owned. What came out was not one big tool but an operating model.

That operating model became the Stravio Stack, and it is the same one we create today.
