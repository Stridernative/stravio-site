Privacy

What we do with your information

Stravio collects very little, and what we do collect has one job, which is letting us reply to you and set up a conversation. This page says exactly what that covers in plain language.

Effective November 17, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out the contact form on this site, or a lead form on one of our ads, you give us your name, your email address, your company name and whatever you choose to write in your message. That is everything we ask for, and you decide how much to tell us.

How we use it

We use those details to reply to you and to find time for a call. If we end up working together, we use them to stay in touch about that work.

Reaching out does not put you on a newsletter or a marketing list, because we do not keep one.

What we never do

We do not sell your information, we do not rent or trade it, and we do not hand it to anyone else for their marketing.

Where it goes

This website has no database and stores nothing you type into it. When you press send, a form service called Web3Forms relays your message to our business email inbox, and Web3Forms states that it forwards submissions without storing them. From there your message lives in our inbox the way any email you sent us would.

If you reach us through a form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta collects what you enter under its own privacy policy and passes it along to us, and we treat it exactly the same way.

The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider, GitHub, logs the IP addresses of visitors for security, which we do not see or use. Because the site does not track visitors, it has nothing to change when a browser sends a Do Not Track signal. If any of this changes, this page will be updated before the change goes live.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we have from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email gabe@stravioai.com and we will take care of it within thirty days.

Who we are

This site is run by Stravio, LLC, a Texas company. It is written for businesses and is not directed at children. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top of the page.

Questions about any of it are welcome through the contact page.
