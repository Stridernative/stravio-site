The problem

Using AI is the easy part

AI is everywhere now, and most teams have already started using it. An assistant here, a chat window there, a little lift on the busywork. The hard part is what comes next: turning scattered use into a business system, with workflows and automation processes that carry real work from start to finish.

That step is where nearly everyone stalls, and we have watched it happen at every scale, from a local tire shop to a half billion dollar enterprise pipeline. Not for lack of talent or tools, but because nobody owns the architecture. That is the gap Stravio closes.
