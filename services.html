What one month looks like when it works.

A SaaS company mid restructure. Support drowning in noise, customer knowledge scattered across tools, briefing prep eating the better part of an hour per customer. The instinct was to hire. The bottleneck was never headcount. It was operational architecture.

In one month we rebuilt how the work got done. Workflows that triaged the noise before a human saw it. Drafts that wrote themselves from real customer data. Internal tools that closed loops nobody had owned. Not one big tool. An operating model.

That operating model became the Stravio Stack, and it is the same one we install today.
