How we operate

Four principles. No exceptions.

01

AI is operational, not strategic

The deck does not matter if nothing changes in how the work gets done. We start from the work and build up.

02

Plain language beats frameworks

If you cannot say what you are doing without buzzwords, you do not know what you are doing. We hold ourselves to that daily.

03

The team that uses the tools owns the work

Vendor lock is a failure mode, not a business model. Everything we build is yours to run, change, and extend.

04

Independence is the goal

Every engagement ends with the client more capable than we found them. If you never need us again, we did it right.

A note from the founder

I spent three and a half years building with AI before Stravio had a name. Not writing about it, building with it, inside businesses where the numbers had to move or nobody cared.

The month that changed everything was a SaaS restructure. I watched a team of good people drown in work that a well designed system could carry, and I rebuilt it. Workflows that triaged the noise. Drafts that wrote themselves from real customer data. Loops that finally closed. It took a month, and the thing I could not stop thinking about afterward was how many teams are stuck exactly where that one was.

Stravio is the answer to that. My partner Brad Fairley and I package what worked into a system any serious team can run, we install it, and we teach you to own it. Then we get out of the way.

Gabe Rodriguez

Founder, Stravio
